EASE (Ensemble Adaptive Sampling Scheme) identifies an optimal adaptive sampling strategy via policy ranking. The software is organized around simulation, policy evaluation, ranking, and benchmarking components for comparing adaptive-sampling strategies.

**Code:** [github.com/ShuklaGroup/EASE](https://github.com/ShuklaGroup/EASE)

**Paper:** [Nadeem & Shukla, J. Chem. Theory Comput. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.4c01488)
