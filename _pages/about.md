I am a computational scientist and Bioengineering Ph.D. candidate at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, working with [Prof. Diwakar Shukla](https://shuklagroup.org/). I expect to complete the Ph.D. in Spring 2027 (GPA 4.00/4.00; Phi Kappa Phi Honor Society).

I develop machine-learning, generative modeling, adaptive sampling, and molecular simulation methods for protein and peptide conformational ensembles, with a focus on sequence–structure–dynamics–function relationships.

My current work includes:

- **Pi-Ensemble**, a sequence-guided machine learning framework that combines inverse folding and protein structure prediction to generate intermediate conformations between functional protein states.
- **EASE** and **AdaptivePy**, computational frameworks for adaptive molecular simulations that use policy ranking and machine-learning-based sampling to improve conformational exploration and convergence.
- Sequence-conditioned generative models for peptide conformational ensembles, trained on molecular-simulation datasets to learn structural distributions across sequence space.
- Machine-learning analyses of large-scale experimental peptide sequence–function datasets to characterize enzyme substrate specificity.
- GPU-accelerated molecular simulation and machine-learning workflows on HPC systems, spanning automated simulation, data processing, model training, and analysis.

I collaborate with experimental scientists on peptide screening and single-molecule measurements, linking computational predictions to observed function. This work has led to 7 publications and preprints (5 first or co-first author) and 4 conference presentations.
