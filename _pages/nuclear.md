I trained as a nuclear engineer before beginning a Ph.D. in Bioengineering. That background includes a master’s degree in nuclear engineering, computational work on reactor core loading, and several years as a scientist at the Pakistan Atomic Energy Commission (PAEC).

## Education

**M.S. in Nuclear Engineering**, Pakistan Institute of Engineering and Applied Sciences (PIEAS), Nov 2015 – Nov 2017

Advisor: Prof. Sikander Majid Mirza

Thesis area: multi-objective optimization of core loading patterns for light-water nuclear reactors.

- Developed multi-objective optimization methods for nuclear reactor core loading patterns while balancing competing operational, performance, and safety constraints.
- Applied numerical optimization, reactor-physics calculations, and scientific computing to evaluate candidate loading patterns and support engineering decision-making.

This work received the Excellence in Research Award at PIEAS (2017).

**B.S. in Mathematics**, National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST), Sept 2011 – Aug 2015. President’s Gold Medal, NUST (2015).

## Pakistan Atomic Energy Commission

### Senior Scientist, Dec 2019 – Dec 2021

- Developed and implemented computational optimization algorithms for pressurized light-water reactor core loading, improving fuel-cycle design and operational efficiency.
- Served as a Quality Assurance Engineer, conducting internal audits, coordinating cross-functional corrective actions, and supporting compliance with regulatory and organizational standards.
- Led radiation protection and nuclear safety training for approximately 300 officers and technicians annually.
- Coordinated technical workshops, regulatory missions, and peer reviews involving national and international organizations, including PNRA, IAEA, and WANO.

This period included the Excellence in Performance Award, PAEC (2019).

### Junior Scientist, Nov 2017 – Dec 2019

- Delivered technical instruction in mathematics, reactor fundamentals, radiation protection, and nuclear safety to engineers and technical personnel.
- Conducted radiation protection training for nuclear power plant personnel in support of operational and regulatory safety requirements.
- Completed cross-functional technical rotations in reactor operations, nuclear-island maintenance, and health physics, developing practical experience with complex safety-critical engineering systems.

## Research to industry path

The same scientific-computing and optimization background later transferred to machine learning and molecular simulation. Core loading work required ranking candidate designs under competing constraints; that framing is closely related to the adaptive-sampling and policy-ranking methods I now develop for biomolecular systems.
