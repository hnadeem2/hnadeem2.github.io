I am a computational scientist at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, where I am a final-year Ph.D. student. I develop machine-learning models, numerical simulations, and high-performance computing workflows for high-dimensional scientific problems. Across application areas, the same principles apply: represent a complex system, explore it efficiently, and rank options under competing constraints.

My current research is in the [Shukla group](https://shuklagroup.org/), where I develop adaptive sampling, generative models, and molecular simulation methods for protein and peptide conformational ensembles. I run GPU-accelerated workflows on HPC systems and collaborate with experimental groups on peptide screening and single-molecule measurements. This line of work produced 7 papers and preprints (5 first or co-first author), including published work on adaptive sampling and recent preprints on conformational-ensemble modeling.

Before this I worked as a nuclear engineer, including graduate research on computational reactor-core design and several years as a scientist at the Pakistan Atomic Energy Commission. Additional detail is on the [Nuclear Engineering](/nuclear/) page.
