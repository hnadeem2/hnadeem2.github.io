I am a Bioengineering Ph.D. candidate at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, working with [Prof. Diwakar Shukla](https://shuklagroup.org/). I develop machine learning, generative modeling, adaptive sampling, and molecular simulation methods for protein and peptide conformational ensembles, with a focus on sequence–structure–dynamics–function relationships. I expect to complete the Ph.D. in Spring 2027 (GPA 4.00/4.00; Phi Kappa Phi Honor Society).

This work includes GPU-accelerated simulation and machine-learning workflows on HPC systems, and collaboration with experimental groups on peptide screening and single-molecule measurements. Research output: 7 publications and preprints (5 first or co-first author) and 4 conference presentations.

## Software

Open-source tools from this research, available as preprints and public code:

- **[Pi-Ensemble](https://github.com/ShuklaGroup/Pi-Ensemble)** — sequence-guided generation of interpolated protein conformational ensembles
- **[AdaptivePy](https://github.com/ShuklaGroup/AdaptivePy)** — a unified Python framework for adaptive sampling in molecular dynamics (`pip install adaptivepy-sampling`)
- **[EASE](https://github.com/ShuklaGroup/EASE)** — ensemble ranking of adaptive-sampling strategies

## Nuclear experience

Before the Ph.D., I trained and worked as a nuclear engineer. I completed an M.S. in Nuclear Engineering at PIEAS on multi-objective optimization of reactor core loading patterns, then served as a scientist at the Pakistan Atomic Energy Commission (2017–2021). That work covered computational core-loading optimization, quality assurance, radiation protection, and nuclear safety training.
