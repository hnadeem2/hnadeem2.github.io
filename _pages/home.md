I am a Bioengineering Ph.D. candidate at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, working with [Prof. Diwakar Shukla](https://shuklagroup.org/). My research develops machine learning, generative modeling, adaptive sampling, and molecular simulation methods for protein and peptide conformational ensembles.

This work includes [Pi-Ensemble](https://github.com/ShuklaGroup/Pi-Ensemble), [AdaptivePy](https://github.com/ShuklaGroup/AdaptivePy), and [EASE](https://github.com/ShuklaGroup/EASE), available as preprints and open-source software. I also collaborate with experimental groups on peptide screening and single-molecule measurements. I expect to complete the Ph.D. in Spring 2027.
